## How I Approach Problems

**1. Understand before building.** Establish the actual problem, who experiences it, and why existing approaches are insufficient.

**2. Design around real workflows.** Build for the people, processes, constraints, and decisions involved in the problem.

**3. Choose technology for a reason.** Use software, automation, artificial intelligence, and supporting infrastructure where they provide practical value.

**4. Work through the difficult details.** Consider reliability, security, maintainability, usability, and the less obvious requirements that determine whether a solution works outside a demonstration.

**5. Focus on outcomes.** The objective is not simply to deliver an application. It is to make the underlying problem easier to manage, understand, or solve.
